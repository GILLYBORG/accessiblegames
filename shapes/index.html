      How the answer is given

      The student answers in their own way (for example a long blink for yes). The adult watching presses Yes or Not yes for them.

      With one switch, any press is Yes. If nobody presses before the wait runs out, it counts as Not yes and the next choice comes.

      Y and N keys always work too. R says the question again. Backspace undoes the last answer.

      Stops a double press or a bouncing switch from answering twice.
