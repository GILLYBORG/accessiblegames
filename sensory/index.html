      Play full screen

      This browser cannot switch to full screen by itself, so the top bar is hidden instead. For true full screen on an iPad, add the Sensory Room to the Home Screen once.

      1. In Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
      2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
      3. Open Sensory Room from the Home Screen.

      For a locked-down session, turn on Guided Access in iPad Settings under Accessibility, then triple-click the top or Home button inside an activity.
