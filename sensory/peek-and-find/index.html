Settings

Highlights one row at a time, then one square at a time. Press a switch, Space or Enter, or tap the board to choose.

Pictures

Use your own pictures for the cover, the picture underneath, and the hidden surprise. They stay on this device only and are never uploaded. On a Chromebook or iPad the picker can also open Google Drive. Square pictures look best.

    Remove background

    Busy photo works on the device. The first time it downloads about 15 MB. It does best with one clear subject, and busy photos may not come out perfectly.

    Play full screen

    This browser cannot switch to full screen by itself. On an iPad, add the game to the Home Screen once. It then opens full screen every time.

    1. In Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
    2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
    3. Open Peek and Find from the Home Screen.

    For a locked-down lesson, turn on Guided Access in iPad Settings under Accessibility, then triple-click the top or Home button inside the game.
