The glow

Many students see red and yellow most easily. Try a few and watch which one they look at first.

The glow slowly grows and shrinks a little. Turn it off for a light that stays perfectly still.

For students who are ready for a busier screen. The other shapes are dim grey and never glow. Only the glow counts.

Find the glow

Help makes the glow bigger and plays a soft sound from its side of the screen (best with headphones or two speakers).

For students who can tap but cannot aim yet.

Follow the glow

The glow rests for 2 seconds at the ends of its path, so eyes can catch up.

Switches and eye gaze

Any key or switch (Space, Enter and others) counts as finding the glow. Turn off if the adult will tap for the student instead.

For eye gaze cameras and head mice. A ring fills up while the pointer rests on the glow.

On the games page, any key or switch picks the game that is lit up.
