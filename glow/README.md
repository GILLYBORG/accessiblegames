# Spot the Glow

A vision activity for students with low vision (including CVI). One bright, glowing shape on a black screen.

- **Find the glow**: a light shows up somewhere on the screen. The student taps it, rests their eyes on it (eye gaze dwell), or presses a switch. It fades away with sparkles and a chime, then shows up somewhere else after a short dark pause.
- **Follow the glow**: the light moves slowly along a path (side to side, up and down, circle, figure eight, corner to corner, bounce, wander). While a finger, mouse, head mouse or eye gaze stays on it, it shines brighter and chimes. A tap or switch press makes it sparkle.

Nothing flashes. Every change fades over half a second or more, and the gentle glowing pulse takes almost 3 seconds.

## Settings (saved on this device)

Colour (yellow, red, pink, orange, green, light blue, white, slowly changing), shape (circle, star, heart, square, smiley face), size (small to giant), gentle glowing, black or dark blue background, dim extra shapes for a busier screen, where it shows up (anywhere, middle, left, right, top, bottom, middle first then further out), dark pause, fade in speed, help after a wait (grows bigger and plays a sound from its side, can say where it is), move on after a wait, a tap anywhere counts, path, speed, stop now and then, switch press means "I see it", eye gaze dwell time, ignore extra presses, hold a switch to go back, switch scanning on the games page, read aloud, sounds, volume, show the count.

## Results

The Results button shows how many glows were found, the average time, and a 3 x 3 map of the screen with times for each part, plus time on the glow in Follow. Results are kept in memory only and clear when the page is closed or reloaded.

## Links for teachers

Settings in a link are used for that visit only:

`?mode=find|follow&colour=red&shape=star&size=1-5&where=left&path=circle&speed=1-5&dwell=1.5&extra=0|3|8&bg=navy&scan=0&voice=0`

Example: `glow/?mode=find&colour=red&size=4`

## Access

Touch, mouse, keyboard, switches (any key), Switch Control and VoiceOver (the play area is one button), eye gaze or head mouse (dwell). Full screen hides everything except a transparent Exit full screen button. When you change css or js files, change the `?v=` number in index.html.
