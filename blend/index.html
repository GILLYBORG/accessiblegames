Teacher setup

Words

Each letter becomes a tile with its own sound, like c a t. For now the game uses 3-letter words only, so longer or shorter words are left out.

The game

Letters always fill the boxes from left to right. "By itself" takes the fewest taps, which helps switch users. Any letter can be tapped to hear its sound.

The Mix letters button on the game screen changes this too.

Help

Shows which letter goes in each box. Screen readers hear it too.

Off: the word is a surprise that students hear at the end, after the sounds. On: they hear it first, and a Hear the word button appears.

Share this setup

Students who open this link get the same words and settings.

Settings

Says the instructions and the whole word. Letter sounds always play.

Voices come from the device, so each iPad or Chromebook has its own list. For a more natural voice on an iPad: Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices, English, then download a voice marked Enhanced or Premium (for example Ava or Zoe). On a Chromebook, the Google voices sound the most natural.

Off: calm mode. No sliding, glowing or bouncing.

Play full screen

This browser cannot hide its own address bar, so the game has hidden its buttons instead. Use Exit full screen in the corner to get them back.

For true full screen on an iPhone or iPad, add the game to the Home Screen once. It then opens full screen every time.

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow). On a newer iPhone it may be under the ⋯ button.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Sound It Out from the Home Screen.

For a locked-down lesson, turn on Guided Access in iPad Settings under Accessibility, then triple-click the top or Home button inside the game.
