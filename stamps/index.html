    About the symbols

    Mulberry Symbols by Steve Lee, mulberrysymbols.org, licensed CC BY-SA. They are stored with this game.

    ARASAAC pictograms: the pictographic symbols used are the property of the Government of Aragón and have been created by Sergio Palao for ARASAAC (arasaac.org), which distributes them under a Creative Commons BY-NC-SA licence. Free for school use, not for selling. They load from arasaac.org while you play, so they need the internet, and a school filter could block them.

    Settings

    Switch

    The stamps light up one at a time and their names are said. Press the switch (or any key) to stamp the lit one.

    "Student chooses" lights up a row, then a spot in that row.

    Helps with tremor or a bouncing switch.

    Sound and look

    Screen reader users hear the names from VoiceOver or ChromeVox too, so you can turn this off for them.

    Play full screen

    This browser cannot hide its own address bar, so the game has hidden its buttons instead. Use Exit full screen in the corner to get them back.

    For true full screen on an iPad, add the game to the Home Screen once: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens full screen every time.
