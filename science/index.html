Science Lab

Home

Change one thing, watch what happens, and check the results in a graph or a table. One simulator for each grade, from the Ontario Science and Technology curriculum (2022).

Simulators

  • Press to play Grade 1 Sun and Shadows. Grade 1. Move the sun through the day and the seasons. Measure your shadow. Earth and Space: Daily and Seasonal Changes
  • Press to play Grade 2 Hot and Cold. Grade 2. Heat and cool ice, chocolate and wax. Melt, freeze and boil. Matter and Energy: Properties of Liquids and Solids
  • Press to play Grade 3 Plant Lab. Grade 3. Grow three plants. Change light, water and soil. Is it a fair test? Life Systems: Growth and Changes in Plants
  • Coming soon Grade 4 Food Chains. Grade 4. Coming soon. Build a habitat. Add or take away a living thing and see what happens to the others. Life Systems: Habitats and Communities
  • Coming soon Grade 5 Bridge Builder. Grade 5. Coming soon. Build a bridge, add weight, and see which parts push and which parts pull. Structures and Mechanisms: Forces Acting on Structures
  • Coming soon Grade 6 Circuit Builder. Grade 6. Coming soon. Join batteries, bulbs and switches. Find out what conducts electricity. Matter and Energy: Electrical Phenomena

Tap a simulator to start. Keyboard: Tab to a simulator, then Enter. Switch: turn on Switch scanning, then press your switch when the one you want lights up. Each simulator has its own Settings for talking, high contrast and switches.
