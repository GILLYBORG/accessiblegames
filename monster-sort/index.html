Play full screen

This browser cannot switch to full screen by itself. On an iPad, add the game to the Home Screen once. It then opens full screen every time.

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Monster Sort from the Home Screen.

For a locked-down lesson, turn on Guided Access in iPad Settings under Accessibility, then triple-click the top or Home button inside the game.
